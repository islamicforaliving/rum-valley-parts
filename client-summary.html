What Your Client (Rum Valley) Needs to Do Next



STEP 1: Set Up User Accounts (FREE - Takes 10 Minutes)



Right now, the site can *pretend* to have accounts, but it doesn't actually save customer information anywhere. To make it real:

1. Go to https://supabase.com and create a free account
2. Create a new project called "rum-valley-parts"
3. Copy the SQL code from a file called database-schema.sql (we gave you this)
4. Run it in their SQL Editor
5. Copy two codes they give you (called "URL" and "anon key")
6. Send those codes to us — we'll add them to the website

Cost: FREE for starter plan (enough for hundreds of customers)

---

STEP 2: Set Up Payments (Takes 15 Minutes)



Right now, the checkout page *looks* real but doesn't actually take money. To accept real payments:

1. Go to https://stripe.com and sign up (it's free to start)
2. Verify your business information
3. Get your "Publishable Key" from the dashboard
4. Send that key to us

Cost: Stripe takes 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction (standard for all online stores)

---

STEP 3: Put It On The Internet (We Can Do This)



Once you give us the codes from Step 1 and Step 2, we can:
- Connect the website to your custom domain (like parts.rumvalley.com)
- Turn on real payments
- Save customer orders to a database
- Send email confirmations when someone orders
